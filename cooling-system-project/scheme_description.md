## 📌 Description

Design of a closed-loop cooling system for industrial equipment.

## ⚙️ Parameters

* Flow rate: 10 m³/h
* Temperature: 12 → 18°C
* Medium: water

## 🧩 System includes

* Pump
* Heat exchanger
* Consumer(agregat)
* Filter
* Control valve
* Valves
* non-return valve after the pump
* expansion tank
* termostat valve
* control indicators
* pipeline
